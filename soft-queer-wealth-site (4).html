Guiding Principle

We are not building an event guide.

We are building community infrastructure. Media creates visibility. The Discovery Platform creates access. Community Intelligence creates learning. Together, they help communities, organizers, and businesses better understand one another.

Our Commitment

Data Integrity
& Ethics

How we collect, handle, and protect community data — and what we will never do with it.

This data is not a product we sell. It is a resource we steward — for and with the Black queer wlw community that makes it possible.

01

Community Ownership First

This data belongs to the community it represents. Soft Queer Wealth is the steward, not the owner. All decisions about how data is used, expanded, or shared will prioritize community benefit over commercial gain. We will never sell community data to third parties, advertisers, or platforms.

02

Transparent Data Sourcing

All data comes from publicly available information (event listings, venue websites, social media, flyers) and firsthand community experience from our team and contributors. We label which type of data underlies each field and use "unknown" where we don't have verified information.

03

Honest About Limitations

Our dataset is incomplete by design — we'd rather publish an honest incomplete guide than a polished but inaccurate one. We flag data gaps explicitly, update records when we receive corrections, and publish version notes when the schema or data changes.

04

No Pay-to-Play. Ever.

Venues and organizers cannot pay to appear in the platform, receive better placement, or be featured more prominently. Placement is determined entirely by community relevance. Revenue programs are editorially separate — no commercial relationship influences community-facing content.

05

Accessibility Data Is Sacred

Accessibility information affects whether community members with disabilities or gender-nonconforming identities can safely attend. This data is always labeled with its source, never estimated, and updated promptly when conditions change. Getting this wrong has real consequences for real people.

06

Survey Data Requires Informed Consent

All survey participants receive full informed consent documentation: what data is collected, how it will be used, who sees it, how long it is retained, and how to withdraw. Survey data is reported in aggregate only — no individual responses will be identifiable in any published output.

07

Organizer Intelligence Is Private by Default

Survey findings shared with organizers are private reports — not published publicly without their consent. Community Insights (the public-facing layer) is aggregated, anonymized, and never ties findings back to individual respondents or specific organizer relationships.

08

Community Submissions Are Treated as Gifts

When community members submit events, venues, or corrections, we treat that labor as a gift. We acknowledge submissions, communicate outcomes, and never use submitted content beyond the platform without explicit permission.

09

Right to Correction and Removal

Venues, organizers, and community members have the right to request correction of inaccurate information or removal from the platform. We review all requests within 14 days and respond with our decision and reasoning.

10

This Document Is Living

As our data practices evolve, this document will be updated. Major changes will be communicated to our community before they take effect. Accountability is ongoing — not a one-time statement.
