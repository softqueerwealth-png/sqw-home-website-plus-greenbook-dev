Building Things That Matter

Why I build

I've spent more than a decade at the intersection of public health, epidemiology, data science and healthcare AI. Again and again, I watched meaningful work stall in bureaucracy, funding limits and long implementation cycles. Products reached the research or patent stage and never reached the people they were designed for.

I'm not walking away from healthcare or technology. I'm bringing my skills closer to the people who benefit from them.

Lifting as I climb

I've benefited from education, resources and relationships that aren't equally available. This isn't about rescuing anyone. It's about using what I've been given to widen what's possible for others. My work is an extension of my activism.

My north star

Build things that make a difference people can actually feel.

Success isn't revenue, followers or launches. It's whether people have more access, agency and opportunity because of what we built.

    Separate books.

    • Each venture keeps its own finances, accounting and legal obligations. No pooled money.
    • Value moves only through documented service contracts, technology licensing, fair compensation or lawful investment.
    • Related-party transactions get legal and tax review first.
    • TMPs as a nonprofit is being explored, not established. If it happens, charitable assets serve charitable purposes, with independent oversight and conflict-of-interest safeguards.
