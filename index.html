Current Priorities

Focus is on the highest leverage work: launch-ready UX, cleaner data, community-powered business discovery, and relationship-first organizer visibility.

Product + UX

Prioritize the features that make the Pride guide easier and more joyful to use.

  • Flyer View
  • Date Filters
  • Vibe Type + Venue Type cleanup
  • Community Perks data updates
  • Know Before You Go taxonomy draft

Businesses

Businesses are the fastest path into the broader community platform.

  • Launch Community Submission Form
  • Identify 20 businesses per city
  • Prioritize Black queer-owned businesses
  • Use community recommendations as warm outreach
  • Build Community Perks pipeline

Organizers

Start relationship-building early because organizers take time to respond.

  • Begin Organizer Visibility outreach
  • Send profile template after opt-in
  • Start with warm partners
  • Separate public promotion from private feedback
  • Continue Community Intelligence pilot conversations

⚠ What We May Be Missing / Current Answers

Metrics Baseline

Analytics are already set up, and we are tracking daily active users. Next layer should include returning users, traffic sources, ticket clicks, event saves/shares, and city-specific engagement.

Survey Distribution Plan

Distribution will happen through promoter distribution to their audience, SQW Instagram distribution, and site distribution. QR codes and post-event links can be added depending on the organizer/pilot.

Organizer Onboarding Flow

After an organizer says yes to a profile, SQW sends the organizer profile template. They complete the questions, SQW edits for consistency, and then the profile is uploaded to the site.

Business Tier Pricing

Draft pricing still needs more thought. Current pilot thinking: Tier 2 promotional reel is $500 for pilot partner businesses, though standard reel value is closer to $2K. Tier 3 consulting/community intelligence may cost $150-$300 depending on business size and scope.

Baltimore Data Entry

Baltimore Pride is currently live and in active iteration this week. Data sprint includes event updates, flyers, ticket links, community perks, business recommendations, and QA.

Content Calendar Ownership

Team meetings are already happening, and user stories can define weekly deliverables and ownership. Next step is creating a general team tracker similar to the Tech Lead tracker, so everyone can see who owns what.
